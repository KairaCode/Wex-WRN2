# Changelog

Format-level changes only, newest first: container versions, record codecs, flags and ids, the helper's feature bits, the tools that write or convert files, and the date a format went into or out of production. Engine tuning is in [STATUS.md](STATUS.md), its measurements in [docs/BENCHMARKS.md](docs/BENCHMARKS.md).

Entries are dated; commit ids of the maintainer's engine tree, which is not public, are not given. The history of this repository starts on 2026-11-12, when the earlier work (from 2026-11-09) was gathered into one commit; an entry is identified by its date. There are no release tags yet. Specification versions: WEX4 4.0 ([docs/WEX4-SPEC.md](docs/WEX4-SPEC.md)) and WNR3 v1 ([docs/WNR3-SPEC.md](docs/WNR3-SPEC.md)); WEX1 to WEX3 and WNR2 are described in [docs/WNR2-REFERENCE.md](docs/WNR2-REFERENCE.md). The entries before 2026-11-09 come from the timeline and measurement sections of [docs/reports/wnr2-history-20261110.md](docs/reports/wnr2-history-20261110.md) and were not re-verified.

## 2026-11-15

- **2026-11-15** Research and tooling, no format byte changed and no file under `c/` changed: the WNR3 Vulkan prototype `wex4/research/vulkan-decode/` ran on a GPU, an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 with driver 621.01 on Windows 10 ([research/vulkan-gpu-20261115](research/vulkan-gpu-20261115/README.md)). There the shader of 2026-11-14 lost decrements of the ceil-form inverse (28 and 50 scale bytes of the two valid ceil-form records of the selftest, the same in two runs; `spirv-val` and the validation layer said nothing); `wnr3.comp` now reads each scale word once per stream and never reads back a word it wrote, and the negative control's line for the decrement follows it. Since then `run.sh` passes there with the validation layer on, also with `MUTATIONS=1000`. The symbol lookups of `wnr3.comp` are tables by default: the scale symbol from a table of one byte per slot, built by the host from the validated model and checked against the binary search on all 2^14 slots, and the weight symbol from one compare of the whole context row; `-DSEARCH` builds the binary search of the first version, and `run.sh` checks both forms. In pairs on the same batch the table form took 0.94 to 0.96 of the device time of the search on 16 synthetic records of the V4.1 geometry, and 0.68 on the 48 records of the tiny-model container. `wnr3_vk.c`: `WNR3_VK_DEVICE` also takes a part of the device name and every device is printed with its driver version; on a device with memory of its own the inputs are copied into device-local buffers (`WNR3_VK_STAGE`); `WNR3_VK_ROBUST=1` enables `robustBufferAccess`; a mode `bench` times the upload, both passes (also by device timestamps) and the read-back against the reference decoder and, built with `-DWNR3_VK_FAST`, the fast CPU decoder, and times two shader forms in pairs (`WNR3_VK_SHADERS_B`). The new `bench.sh` writes for it a container of synthetic records of the V4.1 geometry, through the new options `--hidden` and `--inter` of `fixtures/make_synth_mxfp4.py` (its default output is the same bytes as before). Measured there, 16 records, 21 runs, medians: 1.230 ms of device time per record for the decode and 3.637 ms with the upload, against 24.945 ms for the fast decoder (AVX2) on the 8 threads of an Intel Core i7-7700K that other jobs were loading and 862.982 ms for the reference decoder on one thread; synthetic records, not a model, and no record of a real model was decoded. The llvmpipe crash of issue 21: `crash_probe.sh` with `crash.comp`, `crash_min.comp` and `crash_min.c` reduces it on the CI runner (Mesa 25.2.8, LLVM 20.1.2) to a shader of about 30 lines run by a host of its own on three small buffers, which crashes only when the first invocation of a group of 8 has left before a loop whose count is read from a buffer and whose body searches another buffer, and only with a binding 0 in the descriptor set layout. The first form of the shader is valid SPIR-V (`spirv-val`), gives no message of the validation layer and crashes the same with `robustBufferAccess`: the crash is in the driver. Mesa 26.2.6 with LLVM 23.1.4 and the NVIDIA driver run every form without a crash. The opt-in `linux-vulkan` CI job installs `spirv-tools` and `gdb` and runs the probe, which is not a check. No report was sent to Mesa or to NVIDIA.
