## 2026-11-22

- **2026-11-22** Research and a draft, no byte of an existing format or feature bit changed, no file under `c/` changed and no verdict of a conformance vector changed. WNR4 kind 3, "I4" ([docs/WNR4-SPEC.md](docs/WNR4-SPEC.md) section 6): int4-g64 expert records (1 to 3 matrices of signed int4 codes stored as nibble v + 8, one f32 scale per 64 inputs along the row, "planar" or "pairs" packing; the codes of every matrix, then their scales) in WNR4 tiles. Phase 1 codes each scale as an index k into the lattice `fl(fl(E4M3(k) x B) / 7)` of its block's base B (one f32 per 128 rows x 128 inputs, stored in the record), as a delta from the index of the group r rows above, or as the escape index 127 and its 4 bytes; phase 2 codes the 64 nibbles of each group with (class of its scale, previous magnitude, running maximum magnitude) as context; the tail of lanes 2..31 rides in their state and lanes 0 and 1 end at the 32-bit tile hash, as for the other kinds. Every decoded byte is under a tile hash (no verbatim area). WNM4 model of kind 3: 196,688 bytes. CPU codec `wnr4/wnr4_i4.h`, ctypes API `wnr4/tools/wnr4_i4_tools.c`, CPU tests `wnr4/tests/test_wnr4_i4.c` (round trips over G 1..256 with planar and pairs packing, 1 to 3 matrices, hostile scales and all-zero records; every single-bit flip of small records; a mutation fuzz; refusals of headers, bases, geometries and model blobs; known answers that pin the format bytes), CUDA decoder `wnr4/wnr4_i4_cuda.cuh` (G <= 64) and GPU test `wnr4/tests/test_wnr4_i4_gpu.cu` (sm_120, not in CI). The prototype CUDA helper `wnr4/tools/wnr4_cuda_bridge.cu` adds `wnr4_cuda_i4_register_model`, `wnr4_cuda_i4_batch_submit` and `wnr4_cuda_i4_decode_batch`, and its `wnr4_cuda_abi()` is now 6 (was 5); the other entry points are unchanged. [docs/WEX4-SPEC.md](docs/WEX4-SPEC.md) section 7.3 admits kind 3 under codec 8 on records that are neither EXPERT nor VALUE_EXACT and says where an I4 record's geometry comes from (its tensors) and its packing (record byte 13); conformance vectors for codec 8 still wait until the draft is adopted, and the Go and Rust readers, which have no WNR4 decoder, are unchanged. `wex4/wnr4i4.py` trains, packs (G = 32 by default, `--pairs` for pairs packing) and verifies codec-8 containers from any RAW WEX4 container whose records hold int4-g64 tensors; its tests are `wex4/test_wnr4i4.py`. `wex4/wnr4gg.py`: `Progress` takes the tag it prints. `scripts/build_wnr4_check.sh` also builds and runs `test_wnr4_i4.c`, and `scripts/check_all.sh` runs `wex4/test_wnr4i4.py`. Measured ([research/wnr4-i4-20261122](research/wnr4-i4-20261122/README.md), one run each): 256 held-out records of an int4-g64 export of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next take 0.78333 of raw at G = 32 (zstd -19: 0.8074), every record byte-exact and every scale on the lattice; CPU decode 84 MB/s on 1 thread and 447 MB/s on 8 (Ryzen 9 7950X3D); the CUDA decoder 156.9 GB/s of decoded records at batch 32 on an RTX 5090, every record bit-exact through the helper; an export of the NVFP4 checkpoint of Inkling-2-Mini escapes 90% of its scales (0.88139, worse than zstd -19 at 0.8406). Not in any production container or engine. Checked with `scripts/check_all.sh` on Windows (MSYS2 UCRT64 gcc 16.2, Python 3.14; last line `all checks passed`) the GPU test on an RTX 5090 (CUDA 13.4, sm_120), and under WSL2 (Ubuntu 24.04, gcc 13.3, Python 3.12) `test_wnr4_i4.c`, also with AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer, and `wex4/test_wnr4i4.py`; not run on macOS here.

- **2026-11-22** A validation rule written down, no format byte, ABI or feature bit changed; one file under `c/` changed (`tools/wex4_verify.c`). Blobs that no record names ([issue 57](https://github.com/KairaCode/Wex-WRN2/issues/57)): every upgraded container has two (the old header page, kind 16, and the old entry table, kind 17), and [WEX4-SPEC](docs/WEX4-SPEC.md) section 6, step 3 did not say whether a reader checks them. Three invalid vectors added, written by `wex4/test_wex4.py` (139 manifest rows, 113 invalid files): `unused-blob-of-kind-16-bit-flip-hash-not-fixed.wex4` (`WEX4_E_HASH`), `non-zero-byte-in-the-padding-of-an-unused-blob-of-kind-17.wex4` and `unused-wnm2-blob-with-a-table-that-is-not-increasing.wex4` (`WEX4_E_BLOB`), byte for byte the files 2, 3 and 4 of the issue; the test also checks that the engine store of `wex4/wex4_store.h` opens all three.
