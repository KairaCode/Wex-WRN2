Entries are dated; commit ids of the maintainer's engine tree, which is not public, are not given. Specification versions: WEX4 4.0 ([docs/WEX4-SPEC.md](docs/WEX4-SPEC.md)) and WNR3 v1 ([docs/WNR3-SPEC.md](docs/WNR3-SPEC.md)); WEX1 to WEX3 and WNR2 are described in [docs/WNR2-REFERENCE.md](docs/WNR2-REFERENCE.md). The entries before 2026-11-09 come from the timeline and measurement sections of [docs/reports/wnr2-history-20261110.md](docs/reports/wnr2-history-20261110.md) and were not re-verified.

- **2026-11-15** Validation rule of WEX1 and WEX2, no format byte changed; three files under `c/` changed (`wex_format.h`, `tests/test_wex_format.c`, `tests/test_expert_store_wex.c`). A hardening of the open path: `wex_validate_table` now refuses a WEX1 or WEX2 container whose header field `aligned_expert_bytes` is more than twice the largest 4096-aligned decoded record of its table, or more than one page (4096 bytes) when the table has no record. Before, the field could be any multiple of 4096 below 4 GiB that no record exceeded, whatever the records were, and the standalone store (`c/expert_store_wex.c`) allocates 24 slabs of that size per layer when it opens: a file of a few pages could make it ask for 24 slabs of almost 4 GiB per layer (a failed allocation was handled). The files the tools of this repository write are not affected: `c/tools/repack_v4_wex.py`, `c/tools/repack_q38_mxfp4_wex.py` and `c/tools/build_nr3.py` store the largest record exactly, and `validate_container` of `repack_v4_wex.py` already required equality for every version (it stays stricter than the C rule). The factor of two leaves room for a writer that rounds its slab up to a power of two or to a granule no larger than its record; no such writer is known. The rule is in `wex_validate_table`, so every caller gets it, among them the standalone store, `c/qwen38_wex.h` (which sizes its slabs from the model's configuration, not from this field; no allocation there is sized by a header field alone, so nothing changed in it), `c/tools/bench_wex_cuda.cu` (its WEX1 and WEX2 inputs) and the DeepSeek loader once the maintainer brings the engine tree in step; `c/tools/wex_nr3_tools.c` calls it on WEX3 containers only.
