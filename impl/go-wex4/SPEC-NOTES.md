# Notes on the specifications, from writing a reader without the reference code

Written on 2026-11-14 while the Go reader of this folder was made from [WEX4-SPEC](../../docs/WEX4-SPEC.md) (document revision 2026-11-13, history row of 2026-11-14 included), [WNR3-SPEC](../../docs/WNR3-SPEC.md) (FINAL v1) and [WNR2-REFERENCE](../../docs/WNR2-REFERENCE.md). Each note gives the place, what a reader written from scratch could not decide from the text, how this reader resolved it, and whether a file settles the question. "The vectors" are the 112 rows of `wex4/vectors/MANIFEST.tsv`; "testdata" is the two files of [testdata/](testdata/README.md), written by the repository's own tools.

None of the notes changes a verdict of the manifest: the reader gave all 112 verdicts, reject classes and roots on its first run. The notes are about what the text leaves open, not about a disagreement with the reference.

## C. What the pages do not say they need

- **WNR3-SPEC is not enough to decode a WNR3 record.** The WNM2 model layout, the stream framing (4-byte big-endian initial state, `[L, 256 L)`, u16 lengths of at least 4) and the weight context are in WNR2-REFERENCE sections 6 and 7; WNR3-SPEC says "exactly as WNR2" and "unchanged from WNR2". [HELP-WANTED](../../docs/HELP-WANTED.md) section 4 and the vectors' README name WEX4-SPEC and WNR3-SPEC only.
- **The geometry of a WNR3 record inside WEX4.** WNR3-SPEC takes `scount_m` and `count_m` from the WEX3 entry. In a WEX4 file they come from the six tensors of WEX4-SPEC 5.5: `scount_m = rows * cols` of scale tensor m and `count_m = 32 * scount_m` nibbles. Neither page writes this down; WEX4-SPEC 5.5 only says that the six tensors are "exactly what a WEX3 entry describes".
- **The WNR3 model id.** WNR3-SPEC 3 says "model_id != 0, == WNMD id, registered" (a WEX3 header field); WEX4-SPEC 7.1 gives the WEX4 rule (the id of the entry's blob). A reader has to know that 7.1 replaces the WNMD rule.

## D. What the conformance vectors do not exercise

Found while testing; each is a way for a wrong reader to pass all 112 rows.

1. **Byte 255 in a rANS-coded plane, and scale 255 in a WNR3 scale stream.** The first version of this reader computed `cdf[s + 1]` with `s` in an 8-bit type, so symbol 255 took the CDF entry 0. It passed all 112 rows. It was found on `wex4/out/tiny_raw.wex4`, a file the repository's tests write and do not keep (a WBP1 record of 4 planes, mask 0x0d). `testdata/planes.wex4` and `testdata/ceil-form.wex4` now fail with that bug put back (run on 2026-11-14).
2. **The ceil form of WNR3** (WNR3-SPEC 4.2). Both WNR3 records of the vectors have record flags 0x08: canonical zero, no CEIL bit, so the inverse transform never runs. `testdata/ceil-form.wex4` has five matrices in ceil form and one plain.
3. **WBP1 with 4 or 8 planes**, and the rotation (transform 1) with more than 2 planes. The vectors have 1 plane and 2 planes, `chunk_log2 = 10`. `testdata/planes.wex4` has 1, 4 and 8 planes with and without the rotation, `chunk_log2 = 12`.
4. **Not exercised by any file, here or there:** a WBP1 record whose size is not a multiple of P; a ceil-form block with coded scale 0 (the error of WNR3-SPEC 4.2); canonical zero together with the ceil form; WNR3 chunks other than 2048:512; a record of an unknown codec (left out of the vectors on purpose).
