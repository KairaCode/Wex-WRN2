# Spec review of the parts not reviewed on 2026-11-14 (2026-11-15)

The second pass of the review that [docs/HELP-WANTED.md](../../docs/HELP-WANTED.md) section 2 asks for, in the form of the [first pass](../spec-review-20261114/README.md): the parts read as by someone who implements them from scratch, every candidate checked against the code or by a test before it was kept, the unsure ones dropped.

## Question

Where do [WEX4-SPEC](../../docs/WEX4-SPEC.md) sections 13.1 and 14 and `wex4/wex4_convert.py` disagree, or leave out what a second implementation needs? Which numbers of the informative sections of WEX4-SPEC can be re-derived here? Do sections 5 to 11 of [WNR3-SPEC](../../docs/WNR3-SPEC.md), [WNR2-REFERENCE](../../docs/WNR2-REFERENCE.md) and the GPU decoder (`c/wex_cuda.cuh`, `c/tools/wex_cuda_bridge.cu`) match the code?

## Setup

- Tree: this repository at the commit that adds this folder. Line numbers below are those of that commit.
- Machine: Windows 10, MSYS2 UCRT64, gcc and g++ 16.2, Python 3.14; no GPU used, no CUDA toolkit.
- Command: `bash wex4/build_and_test.sh` (or `bash scripts/check_all.sh`), then `python -I research/spec-review-20261115/review_check.py` from the repository root. Its output is `review_check.log`; a run takes about 20 s.
- `review_check.py` (standard library only) holds an in-place upgrade and a `pack` written from WEX4-SPEC sections 4, 5, 13.1 and 14 as they read after this review, not from `wex4_convert.py`, and compares their bytes with the converter and with the vectors. It runs the converter's own `upgrade` on two stand-ins of the production files (a header, a model and an entry table at the real offsets, records not stored) to get the index sizes the specification quotes. It compiles seven functions of `c/wex_cuda.cuh`, taken from the file as they are, with g++ for the CPU, with the CUDA intrinsics they call (`__popc`, `__vmaxu2`, `__vminu2`, the `prmt` of `wex_cuda_half_sign`) written out from their documented meaning, and compares them with the reference decoder's arithmetic. The GPU kernels themselves were read, not run.

They are numbered 21 to 38, after the 20 of the first pass. 3 findings are left open (39 to 41). No format byte, no rule of a reader and no vector changed. The glossary gained 14 entries.

The GPU decoder was read against WNR3-SPEC sections 4.5, 4.7, 4.8 and 9.4 and erratum 1, and against WNR2-REFERENCE section 10. Apart from rows 31, 35 to 37 and finding 39, it does what they say: the weight context starts at `pm = mx = 8` and takes `mx = previous` after the first symbol (erratum 9), the stream state is read big-endian and checked in `[L, 256L)`, the ident is `{model, owner, g, bytes 24..27}`, the hash covers the coded bytes before the ceil inverse, the CZ check and the ceil inverse run per block with error bits 8 and 4, the scale is decremented in place after `next_scale` was read, and a stream that reads past its end is refused at its end (`s.pos != end`), as the CPU refuses it at once. The GPU checks the blocks and streams of a record in another order than the CPU and returns other error bits; the verdict is the same (not run on a GPU).

## Limits

- One reviewer pass, on one machine. No GPU and no CUDA build: the kernels and the bridge were read, and only seven pure functions of `c/wex_cuda.cuh` were compiled and run, on the CPU, with the intrinsics they call written out from their documentation (G1). A mistake in that emulation would hide a difference.
- The upgrade and the pack written from the text are by the same reviewer who wrote the sentences, after reading the converter; they show that the sentences are enough, not that another reader would read them the same way. The pack was tried without expert records and on two small synthetic files only.
- WNR3-SPEC section 9.7 (`deepseek_v4.c`) was compared with `integration/engine/deepseek_v4.wnr3.wex-excerpt.c` only for its log lines and its owner checks; the engine source is not in this repository. Sections 7 (gates), 8 (open items) and 11 (rollout) were read; they are history and nothing in them was found to contradict the code.
- `review_check.py` is not run by `scripts/check_all.sh` or by CI and was run on Windows only. It needs the files that `wex4/build_and_test.sh` leaves in `wex4/out/`.
- Finding 40's flag-32 case and finding 25's 2^30 refusal rest on reading the code, not on a test.
- The sibling work of 2026-11-15 on the four findings the first pass left open (WNR3-SPEC conformance section, errata 13 and 14, new vectors) was not part of this pass; this folder neither depends on it nor changes what it touches.
